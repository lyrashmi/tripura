The fourteen sound-sequences that open Pāṇini's grammar, and the pratyāhāras - abbreviatory spans - built from them. Select a pratyāhāra on the right to see its span light up across the sūtras.

Pratyāhāras प्रत्याहाराः

● Each sūtra ends in an it marker (an anubandha, cited with virāma) that is never itself part of a pratyāhāra. A pratyāhāra names the span from a starting sound to a later it letter (Pāṇini 1.1.71, ādir antyena sahetā) - e.g. अण् (aṇ) runs from अ to the ण् that closes sūtra 1.
